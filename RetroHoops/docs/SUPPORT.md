# Retro Hoops support

Questions, a bug, or a problem with a purchase? Email **rodlonbell16@gmail.com**. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version, and the Retro Hoops version (shown at the bottom of the main menu). We usually answer within a few days.

## Common questions

**Is Retro Hoops free?**
Yes. Every mode works offline at no cost. The only purchase is the optional **Retro Hoops Live** monthly subscription for online games against other players.

**How do I cancel Retro Hoops Live?**
On your iPhone or iPad open **Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Retro Hoops Live** and tap Cancel Subscription. It stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

**I bought Live on another device. How do I get it here?**
Open **PLAY ▸ LIVE** and tap **RESTORE PURCHASES**, signed in with the same Apple Account.

**How do I delete my Live data?**
In the game: **PLAY ▸ LIVE ▸ DELETE MY LIVE DATA**. This erases your Live rating, results and player link from the Live server. Your offline career on the device isn't affected.

**My career is missing on a new phone.**
Turn on **Settings ▸ iCloud Sync** in Retro Hoops on both devices (and iCloud for the app in iOS Settings). The newer career is offered when the game starts.

**Two-phone games can't find each other.**
Both phones need the same version of Retro Hoops, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth on, and to be close together. The first time, tap **Allow** when iOS asks to find devices on your local network (or turn it on in iOS **Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local Network ▸ Retro Hoops**).

**The game is too hard / too easy.**
Change **Settings ▸ DIFFICULTY**, or set a different difficulty for each kind of mode under **DIFFICULTY BY MODE**.

**Accessibility**
Settings has colour filters, captions, left-handed controls, large buttons, stick size and dead zone, tap to shoot, reduce motion, a text size that follows iOS, and VoiceOver for the menus.
